FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Working With The Studio

Clear answers to common questions about timelines, communication, file deliverables, and project kickoffs.

What is your typical turnaround timeline for a project?

Timelines depend on project complexity. Standard graphic design and branding suites typically take 1 to 2 weeks. Custom responsive websites and UI/UX prototypes take 2 to 4 weeks. Full e-commerce systems or multi-video AI pipelines typically take 3 to 5 weeks. Every project receives a firm timeline schedule during our discovery call.

How do we communicate throughout the design and development phases?

We maintain direct, responsive communication via WhatsApp (03021468799) and email (chaudharyumair799@gmail.com). You will receive structured weekly progress updates, live preview links, and interactive Figma prototypes for direct commenting.

What deliverables and raw files are included upon project completion?

You receive full commercial ownership of all deliverables. For graphic design and branding: vector AI, EPS, SVG, and high-res print PDFs. For websites: complete source code, deployment assets, and handover documentation. For AI videos: master 1080x1920 MP4 files and sound tracks.

What are your payment terms and milestone structures?

We operate on transparent milestone structures: standard projects begin with an initial kickoff deposit, followed by milestone checkpoints, and final balance upon approved delivery. We accept direct bank transfers and international remittance.
